# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in any repository, application,
product, or service operated by DLO Europa SL, please report it privately and
responsibly:

- **Email:** support@dloeuropa.com
- **Website:** https://dloeuropa.com
- Include the affected repository, product, or service, a clear description of
  the issue, steps to reproduce it, and the potential impact.
- Do not open a public issue or disclose the vulnerability publicly until it
  has been assessed and resolved.

## What to Expect

- **Acknowledgement:** Within 3 business days.
- **Assessment and triage:** We will evaluate the severity, scope, and
  potential impact of the vulnerability.
- **Communication:** Where appropriate, we will keep the reporter informed
  about progress and remediation.
- **Resolution:** Remediation will be prioritized according to severity.
  Critical vulnerabilities will be addressed as soon as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This policy applies to actively maintained repositories, applications,
products, and services operated by DLO Europa SL, including:

- PosCloud
- Gobook
- Zaitha
- TPV365

While conducting security research, do not:

- access, modify, copy, or delete data that does not belong to you;
- disrupt, degrade, or interrupt systems or services;
- perform denial-of-service or load testing;
- use social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks;
- access employee, customer, or third-party accounts without authorization;
- disclose personal, confidential, or commercially sensitive information.

## Supported Versions

Security fixes are applied to actively maintained versions and branches,
typically the default production branch.

Archived repositories, experimental projects, and unsupported versions may not
receive security updates.
